# Feasibility decision

**Date:** 2026-08-17  
**Season:** 2026–2027 BIOBUZZ™, Competition Manual **V0** (31 Jul 2026)  
**Decision:** **`CONDITIONAL GO`**

Proceed with **simulation, training, and passive integration contracts**.  
**Competition use remains gated.** Do not describe training-only functionality as competition functionality.

## 1. Why not `GO`

A competition-capable architecture would require **all** of:

1. A legal transducer path (headset, speaker, or USB audio) named in current rules or Q&A.
2. Proof that team-reachable software can emit **stereo, rate-limited** cues on that transducer.
3. Proof it does not modify the official DS app (R706).
4. Proof it adds no extra wireless/stream (R704, R904, E301).
5. Hearing and communication review.

On 2026-08-17 those are **not** jointly established:

- REV-31-1596 specs list **no speaker and no headphone jack**.
- Gamepad headset-as-USB-audio is **unproven**.
- USB DAC on Hub USB-A is **unspecified** (ports documented for HID/gamepads).
- Kickoff manual (12 Sep 2026) and Q&A (opens 28 Sep 2026) are still ahead.
- Official `SoundPlayer` / `Telemetry.speak` are real APIs, but they are **short-clip / speech** tools, and they still need a physical output.

Stretching V0 silence into “headsets are legal” would violate the investigation charter.

## 2. Why not `TRAINING ONLY` as a hard stop

Off-field work is clearly useful and legal. A **possible** future match path exists on paper: official RC→DS audio commands over the **existing** legal radio, **if** a transducer and R202.B interpretation are later approved.

That is enough to keep adapters and vocabulary development alive. It is **not** enough to enable match audio in configuration.

## 3. Why not `NO-GO`

No rule says “teams may not play any sound on the Driver Station.” FIRST’s own wiki documents DS sound and DS speech. The blocker is **output device + legality + human factors + clip-vs-stream**, not a total prohibition of the idea.
