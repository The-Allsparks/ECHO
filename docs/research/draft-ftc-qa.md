## Q1 Headset on a legal wired gamepad

Category: Robot Construction / Operator Console.

Under R202.B and the OPERATOR CONSOLE rules, may a DRIVER wear a wired headset connected only to a legal wired gamepad analog jack, used solely to hear sounds generated by the official Driver Station application, if those sounds are not audible to others and do not mimic MATCH sounds?
